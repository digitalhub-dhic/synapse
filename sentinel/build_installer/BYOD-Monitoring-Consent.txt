SYNAPSE BYOD ENROLLMENT AND MONITORING CONSENT

This laptop is personally owned and will be enrolled for use in the college
computer laboratory. Installation is optional. If you do not agree, choose
Cancel; this laptop will not be enrolled for lab access under the BYOD policy.

While the laptop is enrolled and connected to the college classroom system,
authorized college staff can:

  * view the laptop screen and take a screenshot;
  * lock or unlock the screen and send classroom messages;
  * start or close supported applications and use supported power/session
    controls; and
  * use supported file distribution and collection features.

The installation also adds Synapse background components and Tailscale for
classroom connectivity. These components and their normal Windows entries stay
visible. Installing this software requires Windows administrator approval.

The teacher console may report that a control request was sent without being
able to confirm that the laptop applied it. Ask the instructor to verify the
device status before relying on a release or re-enforcement request.

By selecting “I Agree”, you confirm that you understand and accept classroom
screen monitoring and the controls described above while this device is
enrolled. You can choose “I Do Not Agree” to cancel enrollment.
